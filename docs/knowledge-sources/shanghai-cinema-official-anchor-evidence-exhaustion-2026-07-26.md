# Shanghai Cinema official anchor evidence exhaustion (2026-07-26)

## Purpose

This source note records a restricted public search for an exact map-anchor plan for Shanghai Cinema, Xinhua Road 160. It does not change any GLB, map placement, runtime registry, Recovery/Hold material, tree, decoration, full-map asset, or another building.

## Calibration requirement

An acceptable plan must show a north arrow, a scale or dimensions, and at least two non-coincident subject control points that can be identified both in the current GLB and in WGS84 or a surveyed local coordinate system. An address, an OSM centroid, or a perspective photograph alone is insufficient.

## Reviewed official and institutional sources

1. [Changning construction-permit index](https://zwgk.shcn.gov.cn/xxgk/jzscgl-jgwzdgz/2022/313/64863.html) identifies the Shanghai Cinema renovation project, Xinhua Road 160, with permit number `2102CN0309D01`. The public index contains no site-drawing attachment, north arrow, scale, or survey controls.
2. [East China Architectural Design & Research ESG report](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2024-03-30/1219466040.PDF) describes the renovation scope, including the exterior plaza, and says the former stepped area became a level public plaza joining the sidewalk. It contains no building-level dimensioned site plan or common coordinate controls.
3. [Changning Planning and Natural Resources project meeting](https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col7696/20230331/1233581.html) records the acceptance process for the facade-renovation project. The public page does not publish a survey drawing or a calibratable site plan.

## Result and boundary

These sources support the factual road/plaza relationship and project identity. They do not resolve the already quantified conflicting road-reference translations in `docs/research/shanghai-cinema-exact-anchor-road-setback-audit.json`; therefore they cannot authorize a placement mutation.

The restricted search did not retrieve a plan meeting all calibration requirements. This is not a claim that no such plan exists. The next admissible source is a human-reviewed, slowly accessed Xiaohongshu wide street view or a formally published plan, only when the user is available. Any newly ingested external-research copy requires separate authorization and workflow completion.
