# Fahua Heritage Public Evidence Rescue

## Sources

1. [Fahua Town: a road through a millennium](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_11011983), republished by The Paper from Shanghai Bang, 2021. The article captions a current photograph of the `法华遗韵` commemorative arch at the Fahuazhen Road–Xianghuaqiao Road intersection. It identifies the subject as a reconstructed commemorative landscape, not a historic surviving building.
2. [A small road that was Shanghai's first town](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_1527205), The Paper, 2016, credited to Shanghai Changning. It reports an on-site view of the Fahuazhen Road–Xianghuaqiao Road landscape: an imitation stone arch and four imitation bridges, with the arch at the eastern end. It also records that historical reference photographs were lacking during planning.
3. [Fahua Heritage street-scene copy disputed](https://xmwb.xinmin.cn/xmwb/resfile/2013-03-24/A07/A07.pdf), Xinmin Evening News, 2013. The report documents the same intersection landscape, the arch, one wooden and three stone bridge copies, and an adjacent cultural corridor. It states that the planning team lacked historical photographs and that the district greenery authority was still seeking local corroboration.

## Classification

Observed: these sources add a same-subject wide street context and identify the intersection, arch-plus-bridge composition, and adjacent cultural corridor. They do not publish a measured site plan, arch thickness, rear construction, curb line, or pedestrian boundary.

Inferred: the legacy asset's isolated front-only reference no longer represents all available public subject context. The landscape is a commemorative reconstructed street feature, not evidence that its inferred rear geometry is correct.

Unknown: side/rear structure and thickness; exact road/sidewalk and landscaped boundary; ground datum; a survey-scale footprint; whether any current street configuration changed after the reports.

## Decision

The evidence gate improves from front-only to identity-plus-street-context partial coverage, but it does not authorize modeling, map promotion, Hero MCP2, Identity, or runtime changes. A current survey/site plan or multi-angle measured imagery remains required.
