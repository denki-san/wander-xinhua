# Xinhua Villas 329 subject contract public rescue

## Scope

This source applies only to `xinhua-villas-329`. It reviews public official
sources without revisiting Xiaohongshu. It does not authorize changes to
`xinhua-villas-211`, other buildings, shared runtime or registry files, trees,
decorations, full-map assets, or Recovery/Hold.

The accepted four-member Massing v3, MCP1, map, collision, and Three.js stages
remain frozen. The cross-asset legacy Hero file is retained as Hold and is not
treated as subject truth.

## Observed

- Changning District defines Lane 211 and Lane 329 together as one U-shaped or
  horseshoe-shaped foreign-lane compound with 29 garden houses. The count is
  not published separately for Lane 329:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col7343/20241227/1275366.html>.
- The same official page places No. 40 inside the lane and describes its
  northeast-facing main entrance, south arcade/veranda, hip roof, annex, and
  chimney. It describes No. 36 as a two-storey circular timber-and-brick
  residence with a site area of 905 square metres and building area of
  543 square metres.
- Official images on that page provide one entrance-axis context, one
  courtyard front/side oblique of No. 40, and one front view of the circular
  No. 36. They were inspected temporarily only and were not added to the
  repository; each remains a single view.
- Another official page identifies No. 38 as a Spanish-style villa and says its
  left wall is beside No. 36:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col6991/20251124/1301891.html>.
- The district planning authority identifies No. 17 as a 1925 Spanish-style
  garden residence, with 358 square metres of building area and 609 square
  metres of site area:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col7698/20240208/1254319.html>.
- A community-renewal report names the small garden at No. 42:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col7343/20231129/1249286.html>.
- A planning-authority notice publishes a formal site-plan image for an
  elevator project at Lane 329 No. 67. The drawing contains a red design scope,
  residential-block outlines, and elevator positions, but it does not label
  heritage members 15, 36, 40, 42, 17, or 38:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col7695/20230414/1234444.html>.
- Official address records also place a school at No. 43 and a leased unit at
  No. 56. These records show that the Lane 329 address namespace includes
  different uses and building forms beyond the currently accepted four-member
  Massing:
  <https://zwgk.shcn.gov.cn/xxgk/xsdw-jyjjgxx/2026/78/81029.html> and
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col5751/20240301/1255317/0e9616534c0f48c3920b701b4fb88965.pdf>.

## Inferred

- The safest current stable-ID meaning is a conservative representation of the
  historic Lane 329 garden-residence subset, not the entire Lane 329 address
  namespace.
- No. 36 has the strongest distinctive single-building identity. No. 40 has
  the strongest official orientation and massing description. Choosing either
  one to represent the compound would still be a product-semantic change, not
  an evidence-only correction.
- The formal No. 67 plan proves that project plans exist, but its design scope
  is not the historic-villa compound boundary and cannot be matched to the four
  accepted OSM ways by shape or proximity.
- Official naming supports future member-specific candidates 17, 36, 38, 40,
  and 42, but it does not yet authorize production stable IDs.

## Unknown

- The formal complete member list, boundary, and separate member count for the
  historic Lane 329 garden-residence subset.
- Which georeferenced footprint corresponds to No. 15, 36, 40, 42, 17, or 38.
- Complete same-member side, rear, roof-back, and annex coverage.
- Whether No. 43, 56, and 67 are included in, excluded from, or later additions
  to the historic 29-house definition.
- The product decision to keep a compound asset, narrow it to a representative
  member, or create member-specific IDs.

## Disposition

Public official search is exhausted for the current query set, but the gate is
not released. Keep `xinhua-villas-329` as the frozen compound-level contract;
do not generate Hero or Identity, reuse the legacy Hero, create member-specific
production IDs, or run MCP2/MCP3.

The minimum unlock is a house-numbered, georeferenceable official
plan/survey. If the main window explicitly chooses a representative member or
member-level split, the chosen member then also needs canonical, side/depth,
and entrance/detail evidence plus its own placement and tier-lineage contract.
