# Debi Fahua 525 public primary-source rescue

## Scope

This evidence review applies only to `debi-fahua-525`. It does not authorize
changes to shared registry/runtime data, Fast Mode, trees, decorations, full-map
assets, Recovery/Hold, or any other building.

## Sources reviewed

1. Dobe's official project page: <https://www.dobechina.com/projects/mandr/77>
2. Shanghai SME Service Cloud listing: <https://www.ssme.sh.gov.cn/public/product%21serviceDetail.do?productId=2c91c2876e3f379f016e5e1fb33d1002>
3. Shanghai Changning government history article: <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col6991/20260527/1309976.html>

## Observed

- Dobe identifies the project as Dobe Fahua 525 at Fahuazhen Road 525, calls it
  the group's first independently renovated and operated creative park, and
  describes courtyard, garden, bamboo and fish-pond elements; it lists six
  floors and 5,428.17 square metres.
- The government-hosted SME service listing also places the Dobe-operated park
  at Fahuazhen Road 525 and describes it as a Zen-culture creative park. Its
  text says the building area is over 10,000 square metres, which is not a
  surveyed footprint and is not reconciled with the operator page's 5,428.17
  square metres.
- The Changning government history article places the historic Fahua Temple at
  today's Fahuazhen Road 525. It is historical-location evidence only.
- None of the reviewed sources provides a georeferenced or dimensioned site
  plan, a cadastral boundary, an OSM-member mapping, a courtyard polygon, road
  cross-section, formal service-road access classification, or a measured
  asphalt width.

## Inferred

- The operator and government-hosted records strengthen the address and
  compound-level provenance of the existing local references.
- They do not identify whether OSM way `864847922` is the intended main
  building, which nearby footprints are in the compound, or where the open
  courtyard ends.

## Unknown

- The authoritative included/excluded compound members and target-building
  footprint.
- Courtyard and entrance boundaries.
- Fahuazhen Road's surveyed carriageway width, surface edge, access regime and
  service-road status at the nearest point.
- A formal site plan able to resolve the current footprint-versus-rendered-road
  conflict.

## Decision

This review retains the existing map, membership, MCP1, Hero lineage and
Identity blockers. It must not be used to move or scale the candidate, reduce
the shared road width, or promote the Recovery Massing. Before this external
research can be called workflow-complete, this Markdown needs the configured
LLM Wiki raw-source ingestion, rescan and search/read-back verification; no
external storage was written in this review.
