# Shanghai Chinese Orchestra Public Primary-source Rescue

## Sources

1. Shanghai Municipal Administration of Culture and Tourism, [Cultural-tourism integration in Changning](https://whlyj.sh.gov.cn/gqfc/20230413/c79520d4b7ff4453b70a55986bb67e24.html), 2023-04-13. It identifies Shanghai Chinese Orchestra at Xinhua Road 336.
2. Shanghai Municipal Administration of Culture and Tourism, [Public arts education venue list](https://whlyj.sh.gov.cn/cmsres/f0/f031d7909a6c4b2abb2a05c6d7b5768f/36cf92ac31f234d9004706565987582f.pdf). It lists Shanghai Chinese Orchestra, Changning District, Xinhua Road 336 and its exhibition space.
3. Shanghai Government open-procurement document, [Guole Buzz AI contract](https://sh-gov-open-doc.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/1035IC/2bd1451e-b190-4d59-a575-f3eae1fe4659.pdf). It names Shanghai Chinese Orchestra as party A and records its address as Xinhua Road 336.
4. TJAD project publication preserved in the local reference manifest: [Renovation Project of Shanghai Chinese Orchestra at No. 336 Xinhua Road, Phase I](https://www.gooood.cn/renovation-project-of-shanghai-chinese-orchestra-located-at-no-336-xinhua-road-phase-i-china-by-tjad.htm). It identifies Buildings 6, 7 and 8 by program but provides no georeferenced building schedule or surveyed plan.

## Observed

- Independent official public sources establish the named institution at Xinhua Road 336.
- The TJAD project record supports the compound programme of Buildings 6, 7 and 8.
- None of the consulted sources maps a building number, boundary, red line, or courtyard edge to a specific OSM way.

## Inferred

- The public address corroborates compound identity, but cannot select one of the anonymous candidate footprints or prove that the five-way candidate set is exclusive.

## Unknown

- The exact OSM-way binding for Buildings 6, 7, 8 and retained volumes.
- Formal site boundary, adjacent-member inclusion, and a survey-grade footprint plan.

## Decision

The public rescue does not close formal membership, map, Hero, or Identity gates. A scaled official completion/site plan, red-line/cadastral record, or georeferenced plan tying building numbers to footprints remains required.
