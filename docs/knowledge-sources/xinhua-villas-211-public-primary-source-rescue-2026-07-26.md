# Xinhua Villas 211 public primary source rescue

## Scope

This source applies only to `xinhua-villas-211`. It records public primary
sources reviewed by the main window. It does not authorize work on
`xinhua-villas-329`, trees, decorations, ordinary OSM massing, other buildings,
full-map assets, or Recovery/Hold.

## Observed

- Changning District describes Lane 211 and Lane 329 as one connected U-shaped
  or horseshoe-shaped compound with 29 garden houses:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col7343/20241227/1275366.html>.
- The district planning authority identifies Lane 211 No. 1 as a Hudec garden
  residence, with a site area of about 1240 square metres and building area of
  about 563 square metres:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col7698/20230109/1228439.html>.
- The district housing authority independently indexes Lane 211 No. 12:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col5934/20211207/1158154.html>.
- An official photo-history page identifies Lane 211 No. 1 and No. 2 in its
  image captions. Those two image files already exist in the repository:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col6991/20251124/1301891.html>.
- Another official streetscape page describes No. 2 as a British country-style
  garden residence with a steep roof:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col7343/20241009/1269297.html>.
- The community renewal record names spaces beside No. 5 and No. 9 through
  No. 15, and confirms internal road and parking-space renewal:
  <https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col7343/20231129/1249286.html>.
- None of these pages provides a north-oriented, dimensioned plan that maps a
  house number to one of the nine accepted OSM ways.

## Inferred

- The official count of 29 houses applies to both lanes together, not to Lane
  211 alone.
- The published area of No. 1 may later reject impossible candidates, but area
  similarity must not be used to guess an OSM way.
- Internal road and parking references establish vehicle use only. They do not
  authorize a road width, collision change, model translation, or scale change.

## Unknown

- Which accepted OSM way corresponds to Lane 211 No. 1, No. 2, or No. 12.
- The exact Lane 211 member count and cadastral boundary.
- Complete side, rear, and roof-back coverage for a georeferenced member.
- Measured internal-road width, surface boundary, and building setbacks.

## Disposition

Retain the accepted nine-way Massing v3 and its completed MCP1, map, collision,
and Three.js stages. Do not start Hero, Identity, MCP2, or MCP3. The minimum
unlock remains a house-numbered authoritative plan or user-confirmed layout,
followed by same-member side and rear evidence.
