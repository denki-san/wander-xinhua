# FICS Xinhua 365 Public Primary-source Rescue

## Scope

This checkpoint used public pages from Shanghai and Changning government bodies only. It did not use Xiaohongshu, a user browser session, commercial listings, Blender, or a live OSM refresh. The purpose was narrowly to look for a formal campus member table, a scaled site plan, or authoritative internal-road surface evidence for `fics-xinhua-365`.

## Sources consulted

1. Changning District Government, 25 April 2023, [FICS Xinhua 365 breaks ground at Lane 365, Xinhua Road](https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col8288/20230426/1235290.html). It identifies the project as a Donghua University-led renovation in Lane 365 and describes its historic predecessors and planned open, mixed-use positioning. It does not publish a member-building list, a boundary, a scaled plan, or internal road dimensions.
2. Changning District Government, 19 December 2023, [Transforming the past, building for the future](https://www.shcn.gov.cn/english/col1140/20231221/1250676.html). It calls FICS Xinhua 365 a renovation project in Donghua University's science and technology park and says the enclosed park would become an open public community. It does not identify any OSM building way, road edge, carriageway width, surface, level, tunnel/cover status, or private-access geometry.
3. Shanghai Municipal Government Cooperation and Exchange Office, 24 November 2025, [2025 creative-city cooperation event held at FICS Xinhua 365](https://hzjl.sh.gov.cn/n1305/20251124/0d2aa6bcd55f421998dff54d8d91ad1f.html). It independently confirms a named FICS Xinhua 365 venue and Donghua University participation, but has no campus plan or member/road geometry.
4. Shanghai Municipal Administration of Culture and Tourism, 16 October 2024, [Why Changning's century-old road keeps renewing](https://whlyj.sh.gov.cn/gqfc/20241016/8b63697820bd4b9c9c8383197228e7fd.html). It places FICS Xinhua 365 adjacent to the Xinhua Community Building Center and summarizes its historic successors and current lifestyle positioning. It does not establish the formal extent of Lane 365 or bind buildings to OSM ways.

## Evidence classification

### Observed

- Official government pages consistently identify FICS Xinhua 365 as a Donghua University-led renovation/creative venue in Lane 365, Xinhua Road.
- One official page describes the redevelopment objective as changing an enclosed science-and-technology park into an open public community.
- No consulted official page contains a building-number schedule, cadastral boundary, scaled site plan, internal road edge, road width, surface material, layer, cover/tunnel status, or photo-to-OSM-way correspondence.

### Inferred

- The official descriptions strengthen the identity of the named compound and its university/creative-park lineage.
- “Open public community” is a planning/usage objective; it does not prove the physical status of OSM way `577252268`, nor does it justify deleting a private service alley from the renderer.

### Unknown

- Whether the five retained OSM candidate buildings (`864493178`, `864493177`, `864493179`, `864493181`, `864493230`) are the formal complete member set of FICS Xinhua 365.
- Which photographed building corresponds to which OSM way, and the formal campus boundary.
- The real-world edge, width, surface, elevation, cover, or operational status of service way `577252268` beside candidate building `864493177`.

## Decision

The public primary-source rescue improves name, owner/lead, location, and redevelopment-context confidence only. It does not release the existing formal membership, service-surface, map, Hero, or Identity blockers. A scaled official completion/site plan or authoritative cadastral/address record that binds each building and the internal road remains required.
